## Design Context

### Users
Primary readers are developers and technically curious professionals reading essays on software, design, and literature.

### Audience Technical Level
Mostly intermediate to advanced technical readers, with occasional first-time visitors arriving from social links or search.

### User Mental State in Context
Readers are usually calm and exploratory. During search and offline moments, they may become uncertain and need quick reassurance plus a clear next step.

### Brand Personality
Reflective, literate, opinionated.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial and restrained: text-first layout, warm neutral palette, minimal chrome, and high reading comfort over visual novelty.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize readability and scannability over decorative complexity.
2. Keep microcopy direct, specific, and action-oriented.
3. Explain system state changes in plain language.
4. Preserve a calm, confident tone in error and empty states.
5. Support progressive enhancement and accessibility by default.
